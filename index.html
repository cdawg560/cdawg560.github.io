Make the most of your downtime

Exploring Mendocino

There will be lots of downtime throughout the week, so here are a few of our favorite ways to enjoy the coast.

Easy Walks & Hikes

  • Mendocino Headlands State Park – An easy, mostly flat loop (about 3 miles) along the bluffs that wraps around the village, with sea arches, wildflowers, and big ocean views. You can hop on from almost anywhere in town and walk as much or as little as you like.
  • Russian Gulch State Park (Headlands Trail) – A short, easy walk (about half a mile) to the Devil's Punchbowl, a collapsed sea cave where you can watch the waves churn below. Day-use fee per vehicle.
  • Van Damme State Park – Pygmy Forest – A short, easy boardwalk loop through a forest of stunted, decades-old trees. About 3 miles south of town.
  • Point Cabrillo Light Station – A light walk out to a restored 1909 lighthouse on the bluffs.

Longer Hikes

  • Big River Trail – An old logging road that starts at Big River Beach and follows the river inland through the redwoods for ~10 miles.
  • Jug Handle Ecological Staircase – About 5 miles round trip between Mendocino and Fort Bragg. The trail goes from the beach to the redwoods.
  • Fort Bragg Coastal Trail – A paved, mostly flat path running about 5.6 miles along the Fort Bragg bluffs, with big ocean views.
  • Fern Canyon (Russian Gulch) – Canyon trail to a 36-foot waterfall, about 6 miles round trip.

Shops & the Harbor

  • Mendocino Village – The whole town is walkable, with galleries, bookshops, and little boutiques tucked along Main, Lansing, and Ukiah Streets. Easy to fill an afternoon between coffee and lunch.
  • Fort Bragg – About 20 minutes north, a working fishing harbor tucked below the highway at the mouth of the Noyo River. Stroll the docks, watch the boats and harbor seals, browse the gift shops, or grab seafood with a view. Shops and cafés near Glass Beach and the Coastal Trail make it easy to pair with either.

Beaches

  • Big River Beach – Right below the village where the river meets the ocean. Easy access and good for a stroll.
  • Portuguese Beach – A small cove reached by stairs from the Headlands, steps from town.
  • Russian Gulch Beach – A sheltered beach at the mouth of the creek, with lots of tide pools.
  • Van Damme Beach – Right off Highway 1 with easy parking.
  • Glass Beach (Fort Bragg) – About 15 minutes north. The shoreline is covered in smooth, colorful sea glass, worn down by the waves from what was once the town dump. Go at low tide for the best glass and tide pools.
A few helpful answers

Q&A

What dates should I plan for?

We'll be in Mendocino from Tuesday, May 4 (check-in at 4:00 PM) through Saturday, May 8 (check-out at 11:00 AM). The wedding is on Thursday, May 6.

How do I get there?

Fly into SFO, Oakland, or San José, then plan for about a 3.5-hour drive.

Where am I staying?

The entire property is rented for our stay at The Fensalden.

The Fensalden lodging information

What's the weather like?

May on the Mendocino Coast is mild but cool, with temperatures typically around 40–60°F. Mornings are often foggy, and it can get breezy.

What should I wear to the ceremony?

Garden Party / Semi Casual. Wear any color you like! We just want you to be comfortable and feel great. Since the coast can be cool and windy, bring a jacket, wrap, or sweater you're happy to be photographed in. The ceremony will be outdoors, so choose shoes that can handle grass or uneven ground.

What should I pack for the rest of the week?

Casual, warm layers. A windbreaker, comfortable walking shoes, and something cozy for the evenings. The week is relaxed outside of the wedding itself.
